For **small defense subcontractors (15–200 employees)** who must keep a current SPRS score to stay eligible for purchase orders, **[Brand]** is a **managed compliance service** that keeps your NIST 800-171 evidence audit-ready and your SPRS score correct **every quarter, for one fixed monthly price — without ever touching your CUI.** Unlike enclave bundlers (OSIbeyond, Summit 7) that make you hand over your environment, or self-serve tools (Vanta, Drata) that leave the work to you, **[Brand] does the work for you and leaves you in control of your own data.**

## Buyer persona — "Mike, the machine-shop owner"
35-person precision machine shop, ~$8M revenue, two primes (one is Lockheed). One outsourced MSP, **no security or compliance staff**, owner personally "owns" SPRS. Just got a supplier-security questionnaire + a DFARS flow-down clause and **doesn't know his SPRS score is blank/stale.** Non-technical; terrified of losing PO eligibility; buys on **trust and referrals**; the spend is already in his head ("CMMC is coming"). He doesn't want software to operate or his shop floor disrupted — he wants the headache to go away.

## Objection / FAQ bank
- **"You don't host our environment or data?"** → Correct, by design. We hold the *compliance proof* (policies, control status, evidence, SSP/POA&M). Your CUI stays in your environment. That keeps you in control and keeps you out of the heaviest cost and risk.
- **"What if our SPRS score drops?"** → We recalculate it every quarter against the current evidence and tell you *before* it becomes a problem — with a remediation plan, not a surprise.
- **"Do you guarantee we pass CMMC?"** → No one honest can. We're not a C3PAO. We get you *defensibly ready* and keep you that way; you attest and the assessor grades. (Claims discipline — never imply a guarantee.)
- **"Why not just have our MSP do it?"** → They run your IT well. DIB compliance is a specialist program with quarterly SPRS/SSP/POA&M upkeep — we do that all day, and we'll work alongside your MSP.
- **"Why a fixed price?"** → So you're never surprised by an hourly bill, and so we both stay honest about scope.
- **"Who actually does the work — are you qualified?"** → A credentialed RP/CCP reviews every SPRS-affecting deliverable; a senior DoD advisor guides the program; we're a CyberAB RPO (in progress).

## Claims discipline (keeps marketing clean while legal is parked)
Never imply a certification guarantee. Use **"readiness," "estimate," "prepare," "audit-ready."** The free assessment's SPRS number is an **estimate**, clearly labeled. "We prepare; the customer attests."
