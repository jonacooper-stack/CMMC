## Naming criteria (from the strategy)
- Speaks to the buyer — a non‑technical small‑defense‑sub owner terrified of a **stale SPRS score freezing their purchase orders**.
- Carries one of: *cleared to keep winning work · your SPRS score/standing · proof/seal of trust · steady & maintained*.
- Credible and solid for a conservative defense audience — **not** gimmicky.
- **Avoids** the saturated Secure‑/Cyber‑/Guard‑/Shield‑/Sentinel‑/Compli‑ cluster (hard to own or clear).
- Ideally an ownable `.com`/`.io` and a trademark we can defend (favors distinctive/coined over generic words).

## Screening method & its limits
Each candidate was knockout‑screened with live web search for existing companies/products **in security, compliance, defense, fintech, and SaaS**. ✅ = no direct conflict found · ⚠️ = usable but a wrinkle · ❌ = real conflict (dropped). This is a knockout screen, **not** a legal clearance — formal USPTO + registrar checks are your final step.
