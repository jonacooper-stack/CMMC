# Muster — Brand Guide (v1)

**Status:** working identity for the validation phase. Formal USPTO TESS clearance (classes 035/042) and final domain registration happen before heavy brand spend (see plan; coexists with the unrelated grassroots-advocacy "Muster" and the defense app "Mustr" — different markets).

## The name
**Muster.** Two meanings we own:
- **"Pass muster"** — meet the required standard / survive inspection. (Exactly what a SPRS score + a prime questionnaire are.)
- **"Muster"** — to assemble and inspect the troops (a roll-call). We *muster your evidence* and keep it inspection-ready, every quarter.

Defense-native, plain-spoken, memorable, and it sidesteps the saturated Secure-/Cyber-/Sentinel- cluster.

## Positioning one-liner
> **Keep your SPRS score correct — and keep winning work.** Muster runs your NIST 800‑171 / SPRS compliance for you, every quarter, for one fixed price. We hold the proof; you keep your data.

## Taglines (primary → alternates)
1. **"Pass muster. Every quarter."** *(primary)*
2. "Keep your score. Keep the work."
3. "Audit-ready, always. We never touch your CUI."

## Voice & tone
Plain-spoken, confident, respectful of a busy non-technical owner. Lead with their fear (a stale SPRS score freezes purchase orders **today**) and the relief (we handle it, fixed price, you stay in control). No hype, no acronym soup without translation, never imply a certification guarantee. Sound like a trusted operator who has done this many times — because credibility is the whole sale.

## Color palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `navy-900` | `#0A2540` | primary brand / headlines / footer |
| `steel-700` | `#33507A` | secondary, links, accents |
| `cleared-600` | `#15875A` | the "passed / all-clear" signal — CTAs, checkmarks, pass states |
| `amber-500` | `#E8A317` | caution / "score at risk" highlights (use sparingly) |
| `slate-600` | `#475569` | body text |
| `paper-50` | `#F7F9FC` | page background |
| `white` | `#FFFFFF` | cards/surfaces |

Rationale: deep navy = defense/trust/authority; a confident "cleared green" for the emotional payoff (you *passed* muster); amber only to dramatize the frozen-PO risk. No neon "cyber" clichés.

## Typography
- **Headings & UI:** Geist Sans (ships with Next.js) — modern, authoritative, legible.
- **Body:** Geist Sans / system stack.
- Tight, confident headlines; generous body line-height; sentence case (not SHOUTING).

## Logo / wordmark
`brand/muster-wordmark.svg` — the word **Muster** in a strong sans, preceded by a **four-strokes-and-a-slash tally mark** (the universal "muster / roll-call" count, and a nod to counting your 110 controls). A small `cleared-600` check sits over the tally to signal "passed." Works in navy on light, white on navy.

## Usage quick rules
- Lead every asset with the wedge, not the generic category ("managed CMMC").
- Always pair the promise with **"we never touch your CUI / one fixed price."**
- Frame competitors as the contrast: *"the alternative to handing your whole environment to an MSSP."*
- The free assessment's SPRS number is always labeled an **estimate**.
